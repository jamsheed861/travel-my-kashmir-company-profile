1. Overview

Travel My Kashmir is a leading travel agency and tour operator based in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, India. The company organizes holidays and travel services across the Kashmir Valley and handles selected journeys and tour arrangements involving Jammu and Ladakh. Its services include customized Kashmir tour packages, chauffeur-driven cab services, airport transfers, vehicle rentals, hotel reservations, houseboat stays, Shikara ride bookings, and arrangements for selected sightseeing and adventure activities.

Operating locally from Srinagar, the company combines itinerary preparation, accommodation coordination, ground transportation, and on-trip assistance. Its local base gives the team direct access to destination, route, weather, and transport information relevant to travellers moving between Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg, and other parts of the region.

Travel My Kashmir was established in 2005 and is officially registered with the Jammu and Kashmir Tourism Department under registration number JKTA00003321.

4. History and development

Established in 2005 in Srinagar, Travel My Kashmir has evolved from a localized travel service into a comprehensive tour operator offering full package planning, transportation fleets, accommodation bookings, and activity coordination.

The company's service offerings span family holidays, honeymoon tours, group travel, luxury packages, budget tours, airport transfers, inter-city transport, self-drive vehicle rentals, bike rentals, and specialized Kashmir-Ladakh combination trips. It actively promotes prominent tourist circuits as well as offbeat destinations across the valley.

5. How Travel My Kashmir manages tours

Travel My Kashmir specializes in fully customizable itineraries tailored to traveller preferences, group size, budget, and travel pacing.

Standard tour management workflow:

  1. Enquiry & Requirements Gathering: Capturing travel dates, group composition (family, couple, corporate, solo), and budget parameters.
  2. Itinerary Formulation: Crafting optimal routes covering destinations like Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg, and offbeat valleys.
  3. Transport Selection: Assigning appropriate vehicle categories matching group size and luggage needs.
  4. Accommodation Booking: Reserving verified hotels, resorts, or heritage houseboats matching the requested comfort tier.
  5. Quotation & Confirmation: Issuing itemized quotations with clear inclusions, exclusions, and booking terms.
  6. Pre-Arrival Coordination: Confirming airport pickup logistics, driver contacts, and itinerary vouchers.
  7. On-Trip Support: Providing continuous local support and managing adjustments caused by weather or road conditions.

6. Kashmir tour packages and itinerary planning

The agency provides structured holiday categories including family tours, honeymoon packages, luxury holidays, winter snow trips, and group packages.

Key destinations covered:

  • Srinagar: Dal Lake, Mughal Gardens, local markets, and houseboat stays.
  • Gulmarg: Meadow excursions, snow sports, and Gulmarg Gondola access.
  • Pahalgam: Lidder Valley, Betaab Valley, Aru Valley, and Chandanwari.
  • Sonamarg: Sindh Valley landscape and glacier viewpoints.
  • Doodhpathri & Gurez Valley: Scenic pastoral retreats and remote valley expeditions.

7. Cab services and transportation operations

Travel My Kashmir operates an extensive private fleet and verified cab network providing chauffeur-driven vehicles for airport transfers, sightseeing, and multi-day outstation circuits.

Vehicle categories

  • Sedans (Swift Dzire, Etios): Comfortable for 3–4 guests.
  • SUVs (Innova, Innova Crysta, Scorpio, Fortuner): Premium comfort and space for families and rough terrain (5–7 guests).
  • Tempo Travellers: 12 to 17-seater luxury pushback vans for larger groups.

The business maintains a robust fleet of over 130 to 150 well-maintained commercial vehicles dedicated to serving tourist routes seamlessly.

8. Driver selection and driver coordination

Drivers are vetted local professionals selected for their extensive driving experience across mountain terrains, road safety compliance, and courteous hospitality. Assigned under a dedicated transport management structure, drivers act as local guides and provide real-time updates regarding weather and road accessibility.

9. Hotel selection and accommodation planning

The company partners with established hotels, luxury resorts, and boutique properties across Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam. Accommodation is matched with guest budgets, itinerary pacing, and comfort requirements, ensuring seamless proximity to sightseeing locations.

10. Houseboat booking and guest stays

Travel My Kashmir coordinates traditional heritage houseboat reservations on Dal Lake and Nigeen Lake. These stays feature authentic Kashmiri woodwork, modern amenities, dining options, and dedicated caretakers.

11. Shikara ride arrangements on Dal Lake

The company offers advance online Shikara booking services for Dal Lake tours, sunset rides, visits to floating gardens, and market explorations with clear route durations and boarding ghat details.

12. Activities and other travel services

Additional services organized by the company include:

  • Gulmarg Gondola ticketing assistance and ski arrangements.
  • Trekking and high-altitude expedition planning.
  • Adventure sports coordination (rafting, paragliding, ATV rides).
  • Bike rentals and self-drive vehicle arrangements.

13. Customer groups and international visitors

Packages cater to families, honeymooners, corporate groups, solo travellers, senior citizens, and women-only groups. Travel My Kashmir regularly hosts international visitors from countries across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and North America.

14. Local operating knowledge and on-trip support

Providing 24/7 on-ground customer assistance, the local Srinagar team monitors changing weather patterns and highway conditions, ensuring safe alternative routing and prompt schedule adjustments when necessary.
