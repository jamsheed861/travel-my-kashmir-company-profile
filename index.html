1. Overview

Travel My Kashmir is a travel agency and tour operator based in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, India[cite: 4]. The company organizes holidays and travel services in the Kashmir Valley and also handles selected journeys and tour arrangements involving Jammu and Ladakh[cite: 4]. Its services include customized Kashmir tour packages, chauffeur-driven cab services, airport transfers, vehicle rentals, hotel reservations, houseboat stays, Shikara ride bookings and arrangements for selected sightseeing and adventure activities[cite: 4].

The company presents itself as a locally operated travel business. Its planning and service model combines itinerary preparation, accommodation coordination, ground transportation and on-trip assistance[cite: 4]. Its local base in Srinagar gives its team access to destination, route, weather and transport information relevant to travellers moving between Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and other parts of the region[cite: 4].

Travel My Kashmir’s official website states that the business was established in 2005 and is registered with the Jammu and Kashmir Tourism Department under registration number JKTA00003321[cite: 4]. The registration number and status should be checked against the relevant government register whenever formal verification is required[cite: 4].

4. History and development

Travel My Kashmir states that it began operating in 2005 in Srinagar[cite: 4]. The business has developed from a locally based travel service into a broader tour and travel operation offering package planning, transportation, accommodation booking and activity coordination[cite: 4].

The company’s published service range now covers family holidays, honeymoon travel, group journeys, budget and premium holidays, local sightseeing, airport transfers, inter-district transportation, self-drive vehicle rentals, bike rentals and trips that combine Kashmir with Ladakh[cite: 4]. It also promotes selected offbeat destinations and experiences in addition to the region’s main tourist circuit[cite: 4].

The company’s stated approach emphasizes local destination knowledge, customized itineraries, guest support and coordination of several travel components through one Srinagar-based team[cite: 4].

5. How Travel My Kashmir manages tours

Travel My Kashmir describes its packages as customizable rather than limited to one fixed itinerary[cite: 4]. The trip-planning process is based on the traveller’s dates, group size, budget, accommodation preference, destination interests and preferred pace of travel[cite: 4].

A typical tour-planning workflow can be described as follows:

  1. Initial enquiry and requirements. The team receives the traveller’s dates, arrival and departure details, number of adults and children, group composition, budget range and preferences[cite: 4]. Guests may be travelling as families, couples, friends, solo travellers or larger groups[cite: 4].
  2. Itinerary planning. The team proposes a route and duration based on the destinations the guest wants to visit[cite: 4]. The core Kashmir circuit commonly includes Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg[cite: 4]. Other options may include Doodhpathri, Gurez Valley, Yusmarg and other destinations, subject to route access, season and the time available[cite: 4].
  3. Transport planning. The vehicle category is matched to the number of passengers, luggage, route and comfort requirements[cite: 4]. The company offers sedans, SUVs and larger group vehicles, including Tempo Travellers[cite: 4].
  4. Accommodation coordination. Hotel and houseboat options are matched to the guest’s destination plan, requested category and budget[cite: 4]. Availability is checked before confirmation[cite: 4]. The company works with partner hotels, boutique properties and houseboat operators[cite: 4].
  5. Package quotation and confirmation. The proposed inclusions, accommodation category, vehicle, route and applicable terms are communicated to the guest[cite: 4]. The company publishes booking and payment terms on its website; travellers should read the current terms supplied with their own quotation[cite: 4].
  6. Pre-arrival coordination. The team coordinates arrival details, transport arrangements and the confirmed itinerary[cite: 4]. Airport and railway-station transfers are among the company’s listed services[cite: 4].
  7. On-trip support. The company states that it provides 24-hour support and that its local team and drivers help communicate changes involving road conditions, weather or itinerary requirements[cite: 4]. Routes and sightseeing plans may need adjustment when local conditions change[cite: 4].

The exact sequence and responsibilities can vary by package. A short cab booking, a hotel-only reservation and a multi-day tour package do not require the same level of coordination[cite: 4].

6. Kashmir tour packages and itinerary planning

Travel My Kashmir offers customized and fixed-duration holiday options for different types of travellers[cite: 4]. Its published package categories include family tours, honeymoon packages, budget tours, luxury holidays, group tours, winter trips and customized itineraries[cite: 4].

The company’s package planning covers combinations of the following destinations:

  • Srinagar: Dal Lake, Mughal Gardens, local sightseeing, markets and houseboat stays[cite: 4].
  • Gulmarg: Mountain scenery, the Gulmarg Gondola, snow-season activities and meadow visits[cite: 4].
  • Pahalgam: The Lidder Valley and nearby sightseeing areas such as Betaab Valley, Aru Valley and Chandanwari, depending on access and local operating conditions[cite: 4].
  • Sonamarg: Mountain landscapes and seasonal sightseeing in the Sindh Valley area[cite: 4].
  • Doodhpathri: Meadow and river scenery, depending on road and weather conditions[cite: 4].
  • Gurez Valley: A longer, more remote journey that requires attention to road access, travel time and prevailing conditions[cite: 4].
  • Other Kashmir destinations: The company also publishes transport and travel information for destinations such as Yusmarg and other offbeat locations[cite: 4].

Itinerary design is affected by season, road conditions, travel time, the location of accommodation and the interests of the group[cite: 4]. For example, a family travelling with young children or older guests may need a less demanding schedule than a group seeking long drives or adventure activities[cite: 4]. A honeymoon itinerary may give more time to accommodation, privacy and scenic experiences[cite: 4].

The company also promotes Kashmir and Ladakh combination tours[cite: 4]. Such itineraries require additional planning because of longer distances, high-altitude routes, changing weather and local travel requirements[cite: 4].

9. Hotel selection and accommodation planning

Hotel and accommodation coordination is one of the services included in Travel My Kashmir’s holiday planning[cite: 4]. The company states that it works with hotels, boutique stays and heritage houseboats in locations including Srinagar, Gulmarg and Pahalgam[cite: 4]. Its website offers accommodation options across different budgets and categories and says that its team helps guests match a stay to their travel preferences and value expectations[cite: 4].

The accommodation decision is considered alongside the itinerary, rather than as an entirely separate choice[cite: 4]. The location of a hotel affects daily driving time and the order in which destinations can be visited[cite: 4]. Room configuration and the needs of the group also matter—for example, a family may need a different room arrangement from a couple[cite: 4].

Based on the company’s published package and booking information, the accommodation coordination process includes:

  • Understanding the guest’s requested budget and hotel or houseboat category[cite: 4].
  • Matching the property location to the itinerary and overnight stops[cite: 4].
  • Checking the room type, occupancy and facilities relevant to the booking[cite: 4].
  • Checking availability for the requested dates before confirming the stay[cite: 4].
  • Communicating the booked category and the package inclusions to the guest[cite: 4].
  • Discussing an equivalent alternative if the selected property is unavailable, in accordance with the booking terms[cite: 4].

Travel My Kashmir’s published terms state that, subject to room availability, a hotel may be shifted to a similar category[cite: 4]. The precise property, room type, meal plan, inclusions and substitution terms should therefore be written clearly in the guest’s confirmation[cite: 4]. Guests should review the final voucher and current terms before travel[cite: 4].

11. Shikara ride arrangements on Dal Lake

A Shikara ride is one of the traditional visitor experiences associated with Dal Lake in Srinagar[cite: 4]. Travel My Kashmir provides an online Shikara booking service and publishes details about ride duration, booking information and boarding areas[cite: 4].

The company’s published Shikara information describes rides that can include views of the lake, floating gardens, houseboats and selected points around Dal Lake[cite: 4]. The booking process asks travellers to provide details such as the date, number of people and requested duration[cite: 4]. The company states that advance booking can provide the guest with the ride duration, total price, boarding ghat and booking details before arrival[cite: 4].

Shikara rides are a distinct service from a houseboat stay: a houseboat is accommodation on the lake, while a Shikara is a traditional boat used for sightseeing and local lake travel[cite: 4]. Guests may arrange both as part of one Srinagar itinerary[cite: 4]. Rates, operating conditions, boarding arrangements and available routes can change; the current terms should be confirmed at the time of booking[cite: 4].
