1. Overview

Travel My Kashmir is a travel agency and tour operator based in Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir, India. The company organizes holidays and travel services in the Kashmir Valley and also handles selected journeys involving Jammu and Ladakh. Its services include customized Kashmir tour packages, chauffeur-driven cab services, airport transfers, vehicle rentals, hotel reservations, houseboat stays, Shikara ride bookings, and arrangements for sightseeing and adventure activities.

The business operates locally in Srinagar, combining itinerary preparation, accommodation coordination, ground transportation, and on-trip support for destinations like Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg, and Gurez Valley.

Travel My Kashmir was established in 2005 and is registered with the Jammu and Kashmir Tourism Department under registration number JKTA00003321.
